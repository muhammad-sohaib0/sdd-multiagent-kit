## Post-convergence design change (maintainer directive)

- **Removed the non-cooperative exclusion rule.** Previously: a critic invalid 3 rounds running was flagged non-cooperative and excluded from the count for the session. Now: no cross-round flag or exclusion exists — a critic that fails a round (invalid or transport) is logged and absent for that round only, and participates again next round. Rationale: the maintainer observed recurring free-tier failures (glm-5.2 429s, minimax parse) and does not want persistent exclusion under any condition.
- Files updated: `spec_milestone_2.md` (US-3, edge cases, AC-3), `workflow_milestone_2.md` (R2/R3 branches), `tests_milestone_2.md` (NT5), `kit/SKILL.md`, `kit/skills/critique-loop/SKILL.md`. The engine script was already stateless per round (no session file), so it needed no change for this rule.
- Engine hardening in the same session (parse/latency fixes, see `milestone_3/000-installer-complete.md`): balanced-brace JSON extraction (handles prose containing braces before the JSON), 429 backoff 10s→30s, raw-text snippet logged on final parse failure. The round cap (25) and all-six-fail escalation remain.
